LEO Satellite Channel Effects on Modulation Classification

15 Modulation Types: Before vs After Channel

Carrier: 2.2 GHz | Velocity: 7.5 km/s | Elevation: 45 deg | SNR: 10 dB

Key Finding

The LEO channel SHIFTS the spectrogram by ~39 kHz (Doppler) and adds MULTIPATH BLUR, but DOES NOT DESTROY the fundamental modulation shape.

Phase information (preserved by complex STFT) is CRUCIAL for distinguishing phase-modulated signals like BPSK, Frank, P1-P4.

Classification Difficulty Summary

EASY - Frequency-Modulated (7 types)

NM, LFM, DLFM, MLFM, EQFM, SFM, LFM-BPSK

Unique frequency-vs-time patterns survive the channel intact. Doppler just shifts the whole pattern up/down -- shape unchanged.

MEDIUM - Frequency-Shift Keyed (2 types)

BFSK, QFSK

Discrete frequency hops are clear but fewer distinct features. QFSK (4 frequencies) is denser than BFSK (2 frequencies).

HARD - Phase-Modulated (6 types)

BPSK, Frank, P1, P2, P3, P4

Magnitude spectrograms look nearly IDENTICAL (all horizontal lines!). Complex STFT phase channel REVEALS the phase jumps - this is the key novelty.
