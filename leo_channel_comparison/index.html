🔑 Key Finding

The LEO channel SHIFTS the spectrogram by ~39 kHz (Doppler) and adds MULTIPATH BLUR, but DOES NOT DESTROY the fundamental modulation shape.

Phase information (preserved by complex STFT) is CRUCIAL for distinguishing phase-modulated signals like BPSK, Frank, P1-P4.

🎯 Classification Difficulty Summary

EASY — Frequency-Modulated (7 types)

NM, LFM, DLFM, MLFM, EQFM, SFM, LFM-BPSK

Unique frequency-vs-time patterns survive the channel intact.
Doppler just shifts the whole pattern up/down — shape unchanged.
Even at low SNR, the patterns are robust.

MEDIUM — Frequency-Shift Keyed (2 types)

BFSK, QFSK

Discrete frequency hops are clear but fewer distinct features.
QFSK (4 frequencies) is denser, slightly harder than BFSK (2 frequencies).

HARD — Phase-Modulated (6 types)

BPSK, Frank, P1, P2, P3, P4

Magnitude spectrograms look nearly IDENTICAL (all horizontal lines!)
P1 and P2 are almost indistinguishable at code orders 6 & 8.
Complex STFT phase channel REVEALS the phase jumps!
This is your key novelty — magnitude-only would FAIL here.
